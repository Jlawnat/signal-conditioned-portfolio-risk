**Status: DRAFT for dated researcher review. No Batch 4B run is authorized.**  
**Design identity:** Norgate-only, sector-free, 270 aligned-bar, later-window variant. It is separate from the frozen FMA strategy, V4, V5-U and Publication Protocol V2. WRDS and further acquisition are deferred. The window is previously inspected historical development evidence, not independent confirmation.

## Fixed calendar and data scope

The acquired, unpadded Norgate SPY series first reaches 270 actual valid bars including decision date on **2025-12-02**. The proposed common calendar is **2025-12-02 through 2026-10-30 inclusive, 230 trading sessions**. Fifty-two earlier sessions of the original 282 are outside this variant. Begin with **$1,000,000 cash and no positions or receivables** at the first decision close. The first possible simulated fill is **2025-12-03 open**. The last decision that can produce a within-window fill is **2026-10-29 close**; 2026-10-30 is the final mark, with no fabricated next-open result. There are at most **229 decision/open pairs per policy**. The observation calendar includes no-order days and explicitly missing pairs. No earlier forced-zero exposures enter the estimand.

Use the Norgate daily S&P 500 constituent flag as the named universe definition. Retain all observed members even when signal history is short. Document temporary-line omissions, effective-date uncertainty and current-acquisition adjustment vintage as limitations. The private input manifest must hash the SQLite extraction, adapter, frozen feature source, action register, policy configuration and versioned runner. Stable Norgate asset IDs stay private. No Norgate rows or security-level results enter public artifacts without rights review.

## Input and strategy contract

At each T, physically restrict stock and SPY NONE, CAPITAL and TOTALRETURN bars to dates no later than T. Require 270 valid stock and SPY dates with exact alignment, an observed T bar and coherent positive OHLCV. The synthetic-tested adapter `src/portfolio/ng_sf_270_lw_inputs_v1.py` multiplies all trailing TOTALRETURN OHLC by `NONE_close(T)/TOTALRETURN_close(T)` and CAPITAL volume by `CAPITAL_close(T)/NONE_close(T)`. It checks the acquired reciprocal CAPITAL volume/price relation and fails closed on a mismatch. The normalized T stock close and stop are in raw T price units. Frozen pure technical features are applied to those normalized signal bars; absolute minimum-price and preceding-20-bar dollar-volume gates use NONE raw close and raw `close × volume`. Execution and NAV marks use NONE raw open/close in raw shares. Current-vintage same-date normalization addresses uniform later scaling, but does not prove a historical-vintage feed.

Keep the frozen signal thresholds, ranking rule, MPB exits, A/B/C overlay formulas, integer sizing and all other strategy components except the two explicitly named sector departures. **Remove the 24% sector exposure limit and the Unknown-sector entry rejection for every policy and comparator.** No sector label may enter signals, rank, sizing, order acceptance or exits. The independent research executor's existing no-credit-for-pending-sell cash/risk convention remains an explicit model choice; it is not claimed to replicate every frozen FMA order. No parameter search or strategy-performance-based calendar choice is permitted.

## Executor and action contract

Use the separate decision/execution journal schema v1, preserving S0 before planning, S1 after MPB exits plus overlay, S2 after all accepted buys and sells at T close, S3 after documented overnight actions and before fills, S4 after fills, and S5 next close. S2 is written before T+1 bars are consumed. Hypothetical fills use raw T+1 open with **5 basis points adverse slippage and $10 commission per fill**. Sells precede buys; same-auction sell proceeds cannot fund buys; no leverage, partial fills, intraday stop execution, taxes or cash interest are modeled. Opening buy cash/position-cap rejection is recorded. An unresolved material event stops the affected path rather than silently altering holdings.

Use `src/portfolio/ng_sf_270_lw_actions_v1.py` as the proposed separate accounting rule: verified raw-share cash dividends accrue to a receivable for prior-close entitled shares, become spendable only on documented payment date, and rebase held and pending-buy stops under the existing stop rule. Verified splits adjust held and pending quantities, original shares, cost basis and stops exactly once; unresolved fractional shares block. Verified cash-only terminal consideration converts held shares to a receivable and cancels affected pending orders; share consideration or undocumented terms block. The layer has synthetic tests but is **not yet integrated with an end-to-end Norgate runner**. Numeric provider factor matches alone never certify legal action terms, raw-share amounts or payment dates. No unverified event may be valued at zero, sold at the last close, or silently dropped.

## Risk and estimands

At every stage use `R = sum(q × [max(0, raw mark − adjusted raw stop) + 0.01 × raw mark]) / NAV`. S2 uses **actual T-close NAV** and final accepted planned quantities, with projected costs separate. S4 uses actual post-fill NAV after costs. Keep cash and receivables separate. The decision budget `b_T` governs the S2-to-S4 indicator, including Policy C; S5 records the newly calculated closing budget separately. Use `epsilon = 1e-9` exposure fraction as the numerical comparison tolerance, subject to approval before freeze. A's 4% comparison is a common reference, not mandatory incumbent-overlay compliance.

Proposed primary historical contrast: **B minus A in mean daily positive end-of-day marked stop-gap exceedance above a common 4% reference**, over the fixed valid calendar, reported with both policy paths and missing counts. Predeclare signed and positive S4–S2 implementation gaps for all valid pairs, pairs with orders and pairs with overlay interventions. Report own-budget exceedances, persistence, deployment, turnover, costs, NAV and drawdown as secondary descriptive outcomes. C is secondary and must be compared with B and a fixed-budget comparator calibrated without this window's outcomes. An implementation gap is not automatically slippage or a causal effect. The comparison remains exploratory even after rerunning with PIT constituents.

## Open decisions and stop conditions

Before approval, choose the C fixed-budget comparator and calibration period; the dependence-aware uncertainty estimator, block-length rule and confidence level; multiplicity treatment; missing-date and action-exclusion policy; and prespecified sensitivity for unresolved but potentially material actions. A final protocol must state how an event-triggered blocker is reported, including affected policy paths and denominators. It must not quietly drop a security or date after seeing outcomes. If a verified economic settlement cannot be obtained, use a separately named, reviewed scenario or narrow the claim; no fabricated cash/share consideration is allowed.

The minimum technical gate is a single isolated runner using the tested adapter and action layer with synthetic end-to-end no-lookahead, action/NAV, order reconciliation and logging-on/off tests. The minimum data gate is independently supported terms for actions actually capable of changing the simulated held or pending state, or a prespecified transparent treatment accepted by the researcher. The private 4A.8 possible-signal screen prioritizes review but does not prove actual holdings. **Official Batch 4B A/B/C remains NO-GO until these gates and dated protocol approval pass.**
