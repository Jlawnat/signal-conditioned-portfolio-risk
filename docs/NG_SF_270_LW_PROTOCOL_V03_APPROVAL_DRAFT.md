# NG-SF-270-LW v0.3 — final protocol proposed for approval

**Status: DRAFT; NOT APPROVED OR FROZEN.** No official Batch 4B A/B/C/F run is authorized by this document. Approval must be dated and bind the exact protocol, code and private input hashes. This is a new sector-free Norgate research variant; the frozen FMA strategy, V4/V5-U results and earlier exploratory manuscript remain separate. The 2025–26 period was previously inspected, so the experiment is historical robustness evidence, not independent out-of-sample validation.

## 1. Fixed data, calendar and policies

Use the existing, unpadded Norgate trial extraction only. The common inclusive calendar is **2025-12-02–2026-10-30, 230 sessions**, chosen because 2025-12-02 is the first acquired SPY day with 270 actual valid bars including T. Start every policy with **$1,000,000 cash, no holdings and no receivables at the first close**. The first possible next-open fill is 2025-12-03. The last eligible decision is 2026-10-29 close; 2026-10-30 is the final close, with no invented subsequent fill. There are at most 229 adjacent decision/open pairs. Include no-order sessions and the initial empty-account close in end-of-day outcomes. Do not reinsert the excluded 52 sessions as zero exposure.

Use Norgate's daily S&P 500 constituent flag as the named universe, with observed stable asset IDs retained privately. A member lacking aligned history remains a member without a signal. Require 270 valid stock/SPY dates aligned through T. At each T, physically cut off later NONE, CAPITAL and TOTALRETURN rows; normalize TOTALRETURN OHLC by `NONE_close(T)/TOTALRETURN_close(T)` and CAPITAL volume by `CAPITAL_close(T)/NONE_close(T)`, with the reciprocal price-volume check. Frozen pure MPB technical predicates and ranking use those T-normalized bars. Minimum price uses NONE raw close at T; the minimum preceding-20-session dollar volume uses NONE raw `close × volume`. Planned stops are in raw T units. Raw NONE open/close and raw share quantities govern fills and NAV. Current-acquisition vintage and Norgate constituent-definition limits must be disclosed.

Policy A is the independent MPB research baseline with a 4% new-order planning budget and no incumbent overlay. B adds the existing 4% incumbent stop-gap overlay after MPB exits. C uses the same overlay with `b_T = 0.04 × clip(σ_ref/σ_20,T, 0.5, 1.25)`, bounded from 2% to 5%; `σ_20,T` is the sample standard deviation of 20 preceding SPY TOTALRETURN close-to-close returns through T, annualized by `sqrt(252)`. `σ_ref` is the median over the 165 available 20-return volatility observations dated 2025-01-02–2025-08-29. The acquired pre-study value is **0.14485371463395177**. All policies remove the 24% sector cap and Unknown-sector rejection. No sector label affects signal, rank, sizing, order acceptance or exit. All remaining frozen signal thresholds, MPB exits, integer sizing, position/volume/cash limits and no-credit-for-pending-sell rule follow the independent versioned engine. This does not claim complete frozen FMA order parity.

**Fixed mechanism comparator F:** Apply B's overlay with one constant budget equal to the **arithmetic mean of C's formula on the same 165 pre-study volatility observations**. The acquired value is **0.03865408095327404** (3.8654080953%). F uses the same signals, sector-free rules, account and costs. It is a prespecified secondary comparator for C; B remains the fixed 4% policy. The numerical constants are tied to the approved private data and code hashes. No study-window NAV, fills, exceedances or returns calibrate F.

## 2. Accounting and event order

At T close archive S0 actual state, S1 after MPB exits plus overlay sells, and S2 after all accepted sells and buys. S2 must be persisted before T+1 bars are consumed. At T+1 open, settle documented payments and apply independently verified ex/effective events before S3; execute sells before buys; then archive S4 after fills. Archive S5 at the next close. Use deterministic decision/order IDs and preserve proposed, accepted, rejected, queued, adjusted, filled or cancelled lifecycle events. The final decision session has no within-window next-open order and no zero-valued pair.

S3 NAV is spendable cash after documented payments plus outstanding receivables plus raw-opening marked holdings after actions. For each whole-order fill at raw open \(P\), a buy pays \(qP(1+0.0005)+10\) and a sell receives \(qP(1-0.0005)-10\). Modeled cost is \(0.0005qP+10\) per fill; a rejected or cancelled order incurs no fill cost. The opening buy budget is S3 spendable cash, fixed before same-opening sell proceeds. S4 NAV is S3 NAV less total fill costs when both are marked at the same raw open. There is no partial fill, intraday stop execution, tax, market impact, leverage or interest assumption. S2 risk uses **actual T-close NAV** with projected costs separate; S4 risk uses actual post-cost NAV. Cash and receivables remain distinct.

Use `R_s = sum_i q_i [max(0, P_i − S_i) + 0.01 P_i] / NAV_s` at all stages, in raw share units. Invalid marks, stops, NAV, membership or material action terms produce an explicit blocker, never zero risk. A verified raw-share cash dividend accrues for prior-close entitled shares and becomes spendable only on its documented payment date; the existing ex-date stop adjustment applies to held and pending-buy stops. A verified split rebases held and pending quantities, original shares, cost basis and stops once; unresolved fractional treatment blocks. Verified cash-only terminal terms convert held shares to a receivable and cancel pending orders. A same-asset same-day composite event blocks until its ordering and combined terms have an explicit reviewed rule. The **proposed, not yet approved** narrow rule for co-dated cash-only dividends with identical entitlement and payment terms is in [the CME accounting-rule draft](NG_SF_270_LW_CME_CODATED_CASH_RULE_APPROVAL_DRAFT.md); its aggregate representation is permitted in exploratory diagnostics only until researcher approval. Any mixed or unequal-term composite still blocks. Undocumented consideration, payment timing, successor shares or an affected held/pending vendor action without a matching verified event blocks the path. Do not impute sale at the last close, zero recovery or any invented event value.

**Proposed post-window payment-bound rule, pending researcher approval:** if an official issuer announcement states that an ordinary cash dividend is payable *on or after* a specified date later than 2026-10-30, verify its common-share amount, ex/record date and that lower bound, then carry the entitlement as an unpaid receivable through the study close. Store no exact payment date. The accounting engine must fail closed if execution ever reaches the lower-bound date without an exact payment date. This is applicable to P&G's 2026-10-23 ex-date dividend, whose issuer announcement says payable on or after 2026-11-16. It cannot change cash available, fills or NAV within this study window; it may not be used for any payment bound inside the window. The official Batch 4B run requires express approval of this rule and its code hash.

## 3. Prespecified outcomes and observations

**Single primary historical contrast:** for each of the 230 fixed close dates, compute \(X_{p,t}=\max(R_{p,t}^{close}-0.04,0)\). Report \(\bar X_B-\bar X_A=230^{-1}\sum_t(X_{B,t}-X_{A,t})\), in exposure-fraction units and percentage points, with both policy means and daily coverage. This is a common-reference comparison; A does not have a mandatory incumbent-overlay compliance obligation. A complete valid 230-date paired path is required for this primary estimate. No after-the-fact date or security deletion is permitted.

**Execution-gap estimand:** for each valid T/T+1 pair, \(G_{p,T}=R_{p,T+1}^{S4}-R_{p,T}^{S2}\), positive part \(\max(G_{p,T},0)\), and indicator \(1[R_{S2}\le b_T+10^{-9}, R_{S4}>b_T+10^{-9}]\). Use the **T decision budget**, including for C; S5's later closing budget is separate. Report signed/positive distributions and indicator counts for all valid pairs, pairs with accepted orders, and pairs with overlay interventions, with policy-specific denominators and missing counts. These populations are fixed before results. Do not call the gap execution slippage or a causal overlay effect without a distinct decomposition.

Secondary descriptive outcomes are own-budget and common-4% exceedances, opening-to-closing exceedance persistence, invested fraction excluding receivables, spendable cash, trade count, modeled costs, NAV, cumulative return and maximum drawdown. C–B and C–F contrasts are secondary descriptive comparisons; F addresses differing permitted risk levels but does not make C's effect causal. No sector-compliance result exists in this sector-free variant.

## 4. Uncertainty, multiplicity and missingness

For the single primary paired daily difference series, report a **95% circular moving-block bootstrap percentile interval** using **20-session blocks, 10,000 replicates and random seed 20261102**. Sample enough whole paired blocks to cover 230 dates, truncate to 230, and recompute the mean each replicate. Provide block-length sensitivity intervals at 10 and 40 sessions, labeled sensitivity. Do not report a confirmatory p-value: one previously inspected market path is not 230 independent replications. The interval describes dependence sensitivity under this historical model and does not establish out-of-sample policy superiority.

There is one named primary contrast. Secondary contrasts, C/F, mechanism gaps and NAV outcomes receive descriptive estimates and coverage, without significance claims or a family of unadjusted hypothesis tests. If later inferential claims are desired, a new approved protocol must specify multiplicity control before an independent sample is observed.

If any policy lacks a required close mark or a materially affected action cannot be valued, **the official full-window primary analysis is BLOCKED**. Do not fill, forward-fill, set zero, silently censor that policy or delete its date. Report the first blocked date and cause privately; publication-facing results remain unestimated. A separate, prospectively approved sensitivity may use a common cutoff before the first affected event across all policies, with its changed sample and limited claim prominently labeled. No such cutoff is authorized by this draft. For gap analysis, a missing T+1 is missing rather than zero; the final close has no next-open pair. All no-order valid pairs remain in the all-pair denominator.

Official mode refuses to run unless the approval record matches the protocol, data and code manifests.
