# Batch 4A historical sector gap

The frozen reference `data.py` loads a *latest* S&P 500 constituent table from public current snapshots or its local cache, retaining a `sector` field. The research runner reads the `sector` column from the precomputed candidate CSV, stores it with positions, rejects `Unknown` new candidates, and caps projected sector exposure at 24%. The existing `universe_lineage.csv` has no sector field. Neither the CSV's sector date nor a historical classification-vintage source is certified. These labels are **current or unknown as historical evidence**, even when nonempty.

Norgate explicitly says it provides only the most recently observed industry classification, with no previous-classification timeline ([data FAQ](https://norgatedata.com/data-package-faq.php)). Therefore its present classification cannot validate the 24% sector constraint at each past decision date. The membership series solves a different problem.

A plausible licensed route is Compustat historical GICS through WRDS/CRSP–Compustat Merged `HGIC`, whose `INDFROM`, `INDTHRU` and `GSECTORH` fields describe effective ranges ([WRDS guide](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/documents/402/CRSP-Compustat_Merged_Database_Data_Guide_9efDcmD.pdf)). [Monash Business School lists CRSP–Compustat Merged and WRDS resources](https://www.monash.edu/business/banking-and-finance/our-research/databases), but this does **not** establish the researcher's account eligibility, HGIC table availability, rights to export, or complete mapping to Norgate asset IDs. WRDS also documents historical identifier tables `sec_history` and `sec_idhist` ([WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/wrds-research/database-linking-matrix/using-compustat-historical-identifier-notebook/)).

Before a PIT replay, obtain a licensed effective-dated sector series, verify security-level identity joins including delisted and multiple classes, compare sector taxonomy with the reference's labels, test coverage across 282 dates, and predefine behavior for missing classifications. Until then **HISTORICAL_SECTOR_READY = BLOCKED**. Removing or silently backfilling the 24% rule would change the strategy.
