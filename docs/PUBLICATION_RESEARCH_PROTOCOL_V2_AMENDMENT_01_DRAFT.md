# Publication Research Protocol V2 — Review Amendment 01

**Status:** Proposed amendments; not approved or preregistered.

## A. Research Positioning

The study investigates the implementation and economic consequences of portfolio-level stop-gap risk-budget overlays in a signal-driven equity trading system.

The central contribution sought is an empirically validated account of the gap between planned risk-budget compliance and observed post-execution exposure, together with the resulting portfolio-path and implementation consequences.

No novelty, statistical effect or investment advantage is presumed.

## B. Primary Policy Comparison

The principal comparison is Policy B versus Policy A under the same point-in-time investment universe, candidate signals, order execution engine, trading cost model and corporate-action rules.

The primary outcome proposed for future confirmation is the mean positive exceedance above a common 4% marked stop-gap exposure reference.

The comparison must be evaluated over a prespecified period using a prespecified dependence-aware statistical procedure.

## C. Implementation Mechanism

The key mechanistic outcome is the signed and positive-part difference between risk calculated after the actual decision-time planned trades and risk measured immediately after the corresponding next-opening simulated fills.

The measurement must use archived decision-time plan records.

Execution timing, price movements, order quantity differences and NAV denominator effects must not be conflated without a justified decomposition.

Report the distribution, exceedance frequency, duration and uncertainty rather than relying on selected case studies.

## D. Dynamic Policy Comparison

Policy C must be assessed against Policy B and a prespecified fixed-budget comparator designed to address differences in permitted risk levels.

The comparator must be calibrated without using the confirmation period.

Higher capital deployment or fewer own-budget exceedance days are not sufficient evidence of improved economic performance.

## E. Economic Mechanisms

Evaluate, as prespecified secondary or exploratory analyses:

1. Potential unrealized-profit giveback exposure when initial stops remain fixed.
2. Portfolio-path changes induced by overlay sales.
3. Trading frequency, turnover and implementation costs.
4. Realized NAV and drawdown differences.
5. Signal availability versus actual executable opportunities.

Separate mathematical properties of the risk measure from empirically established trading effects.

## F. Data and Validation Gates

No PIT-corrected main experiment may be certified until material issues involving historical membership, signal lookbacks, price adjustments, corporate actions, terminal security treatment, historical sector classifications and executor consistency have been resolved or covered by an explicitly approved alternative research design.

The previously inspected 282-session period remains exploratory regardless of corrections to its data source.

## G. Statistical Integrity

Define the primary estimand, analysis population, observation calendar, comparison, uncertainty method, multiplicity handling and exclusion rules before confirmation data are observed.

Maintain separate records for development, historical robustness and genuinely independent prospective confirmation.

Report null, negative and inconclusive findings without post-hoc redefining success.

## H. Approval

These amendments require researcher review and a dated frozen protocol version before Batch 4B begins.

Any subsequent deviations must be recorded prospectively with an explicit scientific justification.
