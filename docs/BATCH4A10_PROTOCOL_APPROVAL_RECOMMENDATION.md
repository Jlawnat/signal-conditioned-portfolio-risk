# Batch 4A.10 — protocol approval recommendation

**Recommendation: approve v0.3 only after resolving the private material-event gate and reviewing the hash-bound record.** This note is a recommendation, not researcher approval or a freeze. The 230-session 2025-12-02–2026-10-30 window was already inspected and supports an exploratory historical comparison only.

The [v0.3 protocol](NG_SF_270_LW_PROTOCOL_V03_APPROVAL_DRAFT.md) is sufficiently precise for the intended analysis. Its single primary estimate is the paired 230-close mean difference B minus A in positive risk exposure above the **same 4% reference**: `mean[max(R_B,close − .04, 0) − max(R_A,close − .04, 0)]`. A is an independent baseline; the common reference does not imply that A must satisfy B's incumbent overlay. A missing material mark or event on either path blocks this full-window estimate; no date or security may be dropped after seeing results.

The implementation-gap estimate is `R_S4(T+1 open, post-fill) − R_S2(T close, accepted plan)` for each valid adjacent decision/open pair. Report its signed value, positive part and crossing of the **T decision budget**, with denominators for all valid pairs, pairs with accepted orders and pairs with overlay interventions. There are at most 229 pairs; the final close has none. This gap includes overnight mark and action changes as well as costs and fills. It is not by itself a causal slippage estimate. No-order pairs remain in the all-pair population.

Policy C uses the prespecified 2%–5% volatility-scaled budget. B remains the fixed 4% comparator. Optional F applies B's overlay at the pre-study mean of C's budget, **0.03865408095327404**; this is feasible with the acquired pre-study SPY observations and is a secondary mechanism check. C–B and C–F are descriptive, not additional primary tests or causal estimates.

For the sole primary paired series, use the stated 95% circular moving-block bootstrap percentile interval (20-session blocks, 10,000 replicates, seed 20261102), with 10/40-session sensitivity intervals. The historical interval describes dependence sensitivity for this observed path, not independent out-of-sample confirmation. No confirmatory p-value or unadjusted secondary significance claims. If independent-sample inference is later desired, prespecify its multiplicity control before observing that sample.

Opening NAV includes spendable cash, outstanding documented receivables and holdings marked at raw open after verified actions. A buy uses opening spendable cash fixed before same-open sell proceeds. Whole-order fills pay 5 basis points adverse slippage plus $10 commission; a rejected or cancelled order pays no fill cost. S4 NAV is S3 NAV less fill costs at the same opening marks. S2 risk uses actual closing NAV and S4 risk post-cost opening NAV, both in raw-share stop units. Verified dividends accrue for entitled prior-close shares and become spendable on documented payment date; splits rebase held/pending shares and stops; cash-only terminal terms convert holdings to receivables and cancel pending orders. Unsupported or unverified material event terms, payment timing, successor consideration, composite ordering or missing marks stop the path. The protocol authorizes neither imputation nor a post hoc cutoff.
