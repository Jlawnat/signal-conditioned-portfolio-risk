# Batch 4A corporate-action and delisting audit

| Topic | Batch 4A classification | Evidence and remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary dividend entitlement | PARTIAL | Norgate `Dividend` appears at the prior close to ex-date and varies by adjustment mode ([Python documentation](https://pypi.org/project/norgatedata/)). Compare event amounts/dates against the existing provider manifest and curated USB/CAT register; payment dates are not supplied by this series. |
| Special distributions | PARTIAL | CAPITAL versus CAPITALSPECIAL isolates adjustment treatment and residual `Dividend` values. Cash/stock consideration and tax character need independent event documents. |
| Splits and capital reconstructions | PARTIAL | `capital_event_timeseries()` flags a capital event, but does not give the split ratio or full event terms. Compare NONE/CAPITAL price and volume factors around flags, then source ratios before quantity changes. |
| Mergers, share conversions and delisting economics | MISSING | Last quoted date and delisted-database status are observable; consideration, conversion ratio and settlement date need independent notices/filings. Do not liquidate automatically at the last close. |
| Symbol/asset changes | PARTIAL | Stable Norgate asset IDs are extracted; effective-dated ticker and predecessor/successor mappings are not. |
| Payment calendar | NOT_SUPPORTED | Norgate's price/dividend/flag views do not provide a complete payment calendar. |

The full private comparison found **12 capital-event flags** and 12 >1% capital-adjustment factor jumps in the study window. None of those jumps was flagged on the jump date, but all 12 were flagged at the **previous trading close**, consistent with Norgate's event timing. This supports flag alignment, not split-ratio or consideration verification. The 17 large raw close moves and 15 factor jumps across the broader extract range remain anomaly triage items.

Against the earlier market-bundle manifest, the script inspected 3,874 legacy dividend records: 1,758 were outside the study window; 387 had symbols outside the Norgate candidate set; among comparable in-window candidate records, **1,700 amounts matched within $0.01 and 29 differed**. The 29 exceptions span 21 securities and 16 ex-dates, with a maximum absolute per-share difference of $6.1751. Norgate's CAPITALSPECIAL `Dividend` field represents ordinary residual dividends, so classification of special distributions is a possible explanation, not a verified resolution. The two separately curated USB/CAT amounts matched Norgate within $0.01. The private exception file is `restricted_norgate/batch4a_actions_v2_20261102.json`; it must not be published as source data.

Of the **16** candidates in Norgate's delisted database, **14** have price bars within this 2025 onward extraction range. Two were observed by the researcher's wider historical query but predate this requested range. Among the 14, **13** have membership 1 on their last quoted bar. That is a terminal-economics risk, not proof they would be held by a future strategy. No merger cash, conversion shares or delisting return was inferred.

Norgate's [data FAQ](https://norgatedata.com/data-package-faq.php) says it does not directly provide complete corporate-action event details. The capital-event flag is an event detector, not evidence of ratio or consideration. The existing Batch 3 audit found 1,929 provider dividend entries and 23 splits in the earlier market bundle, with only USB and CAT payment dates independently curated. Those counts are from the **old** bundle, not a Norgate reconciliation. The former V5-U receivables remain provisional and no old result has changed.

The new sample has AAPL dividends in the NONE, CAPITAL and CAPITALSPECIAL views, and no dividend values in TOTALRETURN, consistent with mode-dependent omission. It has no capital-event flags in the two-security sample. The delisted sample had price observations but no membership 1 during the requested 2025-01-02 onward range; this does not contradict the prior wider-period observation of 34 constituent days. Full-run delisted and factor checks are reported separately in the member coverage/data-quality reports.

**Research consequence:** any possible open position after a last quoted date must be placed on a terminal-event review list. If cash or share consideration cannot be documented, its accounting is unresolved and that security's portfolio replay is materially limited. Batch 4A does not run a position path, so it cannot certify which hypothetical PIT positions would be exposed.
