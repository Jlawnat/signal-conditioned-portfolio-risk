## Reviewer-facing self-check

- **Clarity:** `R`, `D`, `Q`, S2–S5, common-reference excess and policy budget are defined before numerical use.
- **Flow:** the paper moves from the locked comparison to execution gaps, capital use, costs, robustness and limits; no secondary result is presented as a new primary test.
- **Terminology:** Table 1 and narrative label basis points of NAV exposure separately from return percentage points and transaction-cost basis points; first close is `initial`, later closes are S5.
- **Unsupported claims:** no live profitability, statistical return superiority, causal overlay effect, or independent holdout claim appears.
- **Missing evidence:** candidate-level rejection reasons, alternative corporate-action timing, market impact and external validation remain explicitly unresolved.
