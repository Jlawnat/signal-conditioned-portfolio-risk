# Manuscript V4 — outstanding issues for researcher review

These are review items, not authorization to start further research or alter frozen files.

## Scientific and evidentiary issues

1. **Scope of contribution.** Confirm that the intended journal audience values staged implementation and evidence transparency as the principal contribution. The study does not show a profitable strategy or causal overlay benefit.
2. **Previously inspected window.** The 230-session 2025–2026 period is exploratory. Confirm the precise wording of “locked primary” so readers do not mistake protocol locking for an independent holdout.
3. **Temporal concentration.** June 2026 contributes about 40% of the signed B−A primary estimate. The draft reports this next to the interval; assess whether a journal reviewer would require a prospective independent window before stronger claims.
4. **Risk proxy validity.** The stop-gap expression is not VaR, expected shortfall or realized loss. No observed tail-loss calibration is available; retain the narrow “modeled exposure” language.
5. **Economic identification.** The B−A deployment/\(Q\) split is exact accounting, not an overlay-effect decomposition. Full candidate-level records and counterfactual policy paths are absent. Avoid diversification, cost-causation and return-superiority claims.
6. **Data and execution model.** Norgate acquisition vintage, constituent definition, stylized next-open fills, no market impact, corporate-action source-precision judgments and licensing constrain external validity and replication.
7. **Absolute performance.** All four simulated cumulative returns are negative. Keep this prominent in the abstract/results/discussion and consider whether the target journal expects an independently validated economic use case.
