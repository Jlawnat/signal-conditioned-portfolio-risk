# Signal-Conditioned Portfolio Risk

**Independent Quantitative Finance Research**

## Research Topic

Signal Scarcity, Dynamic Risk Budgets, and Portfolio
Constraints: An Empirical Study of Capital Allocation
Efficiency — Evidence from a Momentum–Pullback–Breakout
Equity Strategy.

## Research Objective

Investigate how scarcity of eligible trading signals
interacts with position limits, portfolio risk budgets
and capital deployment constraints.

The research will examine whether dynamic risk budgeting
and auxiliary allocation policies improve capital
efficiency without compromising risk-adjusted returns.

## Research Status

This repository is a work in progress.

### Preliminary research

- Historical MPB signal identification
- Signal differential verification
- Analytical capacity upper bounds
- Risk/deployment feasibility surfaces
- Independent portfolio accounting tests
- T+1 price and execution-model audits
- Single-asset multi-session accounting verification
- Multi-asset software pilot

### Current limitation

The multi-asset pilot stopped at a corporate-action
handling boundary. A complete validated multi-period
strategy backtest has NOT yet been produced.

## Data and Reproducibility

Raw historical market data, account statements,
credentials and competition account records are not
included in this repository.

The source code is preserved, but some scripts still
reference the original local research environment.
A portable data interface and complete environment
lockfile remain future engineering tasks.

## Important Distinctions

- Software validation is not strategy performance evidence.
- Simulated orders are not actual investment executions.
- Research hypotheses are not established empirical results.
- The historical equity universe uses a fixed contemporary
  constituent list and may suffer survivorship bias.
- Original competition source and account state remain
  separate from this research repository.
